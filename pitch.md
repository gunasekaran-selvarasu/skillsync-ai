## 📌 Executive Summary

Every year, millions of engineering students graduate, yet **over 60% are filtered out in initial technical screening rounds**. College Placement Offices (TPOs) struggle with manual spreadsheets, students learn outdated topics without clear direction, and faculty lack visibility into candidate readiness until it's too late.

**SkillSync AI** solves this systemic mismatch. It is a multi-tenant institutional SaaS platform that unifies **Students**, **Faculty Mentors**, and **Placement Directors (TPO)** into a single intelligence network.

> **Our Guiding Principle:**
> *"Where am I now? → Where do I want to go? → What am I missing? → What should I do next?"*

### 1. For Students: Autonomous Career Twin & Actionable Steps
- **AI Career Twin**: A living digital profile tracking verified competencies across 5 evidence tiers (self-report, resume, projects, standardized assessments, and practical challenges).
- **"What Should I Learn Next?"**: Instead of overwhelming students with 50 topics, AI selects the **single highest-leverage skill** needed to unlock their target role.
- **Dynamic Phased Roadmap**: An interactive, versioned roadmap that automatically adapts as tasks are completed.
- **Resume Studio & ATS Analyzer**: Upload resumes in PDF or DOCX to receive instant ATS keyword scores and section-by-section improvement advice.
- **Interview Simulator & Verification**: On-demand technical, coding, and behavioral interview rooms with instant feedback and scoring.
- **Career "What-If" Simulator**: Allows students to simulate switching career paths (e.g. Full Stack to Cloud/DevOps) to preview transferable skills and transition timelines.

### 2. For Faculty Mentors: Proactive Cohort Guidance
- **Cohort Readiness Heatmaps**: Real-time view of mentee progress across departments.
- **Early Intervention Flags**: Automatically flags students whose skill evidence or CGPA puts them at risk before campus drives commence.
- **Direct Feedback Dispatch**: Mentors send actionable milestones directly to students' dashboards.

### 3. For TPO & College Leadership: Placement Intelligence
- **1-Click Candidate Eligibility Matching**: When corporate partners (e.g. Google, Amazon, Microsoft) announce placement drives, TPOs filter and rank candidates by verified skills and CGPA in seconds.
- **Recruitment Drive Management**: Track student applications, interview shortlists, and placement offers.
- **Accreditation & Governance Reports**: Institutional analytics ready for NAAC, NIRF, and ABET compliance audits.

## 🔒 Enterprise-Grade Architecture & Security

- **Multi-Tenant College Isolation**: Strict separation of student and college records backed by JWT access tokens.
- **MongoDB Scalability**: Document storage optimized with high-performance compound indexing.
- **Dual AI Engine**: Cloud-native Claude 3.5 Sonnet support alongside local on-premise Ollama execution with deterministic offline fallbacks.
- **Zero Sensitive Data Exposure**: All credentials, tokens, and keys are protected in environment configuration files.
